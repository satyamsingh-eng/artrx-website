Hi Thanvi and Dr. Sushma! After our call, I took time to go through every detail you shared. I completely understand what ArtRX means to you: it's a quiet, caring initiative to help hospitalized children and senior citizens feel comfort, joy, and hope through drawing.

01 · What We Discussed

Your Vision & Design Choices

During our call, you shared how you want the website to look and feel. Here is what we locked in based on Thanvi's choices:

CORE MISSION

Comfort & Emotional Relief Through Art

ArtRX gives guided drawing pads (*"Draw Your Family"*, *"Favorite Food"*) to children in hospitals and leads art sessions for seniors in nursing homes to bring smiles and comfort.

COLORS YOU CHOSE

Beige, Sage Green & Healing Navy

Thanvi picked a warm, peaceful color palette: Soft Beige (#FAF6F0) background, Healing Sage Green (#2F7A5C) accents, and Healing Navy (#1A365D) text.

GALLERY LAYOUT

Templates vs. Real Patient Work

The gallery will feature 2 simple buttons: one for Guided Drawing Templates (Thanvi's drawing pads) and one for Patient Artwork (drawings created by kids and seniors).

SIMPLE CONTACT FORM

Inquiries, Volunteers & Facilities

A friendly contact page where visitors can choose to ask a general question, sign up as a student volunteer, or request drawing pads for a hospital or senior center.

03 · The New Website Layout

How the Website Will Be Organized

Here is how we are building the new website so it's super simple and fun for anyone to visit:

1. HOME PAGE

  • Friendly Welcoming Header: Clear intro explaining how drawing brings comfort.
  • Doctor Quote Card: Featuring Dr. Venugopal's quote (*Stars Surgical Suites*).
  • Our Two Pillars: Cards for Pediatric Hospital Care + Senior Care Workshops.
  • About Thanvi: Her story, high school leadership, and research accomplishments.

2. GALLERY PAGE

  • Two Simple Buttons: "Guided Templates" vs. "Patient Artwork".
  • Guided Templates: Shows Thanvi's prompt pads (*"Draw Your Family"*, *"Favorite Food"*).
  • Patient Artwork: Shows real drawings created by kids in hospitals and seniors in nursing homes.

3. GET INVOLVED

  • Student Volunteers: Easy information for high school friends who want to help.
  • Future Club Guides: Simple guides for friends in other high schools who want to start an ArtRX club.

4. CONTACT PAGE

  • Simple Choice Form: General Inquiries, Student Volunteer Interest, or Hospital Request.
  • Direct Email: Messages go straight to `artrx39@gmail.com`.
  • Social Media Links: Links to official Instagram & Facebook pages in the footer.
