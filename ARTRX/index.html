About the Founder

Thanvi Suvva founded ArtRX to give hospitalized children and nursing home residents a creative voice when words fail during difficult medical circumstances.

Combining her deep dedication to community service with medical research, Thanvi leads outreach across hospitals and senior care facilities throughout New York.

  • Cancer Research Scholar at Weill Cornell Medical Center
  • Limb Regeneration Research at Harvard University (Birol Lab)
  • Scholastic Art & Writing Gold Key Award Winner
  • Jericho High School Boys' Badminton Team Manager
