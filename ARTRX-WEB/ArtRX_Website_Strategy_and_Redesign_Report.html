EXECUTIVE VISION Celebrating ArtRX's Mission

Transforming an inspiring local movement into a national non-profit platform.

ArtRX is conducting genuine, heart-centered work by delivering illustration pads to hospitalized children and leading art workshops for senior citizens in nursing homes throughout New York. Thanvi's academic credentials—from cancer research at Weill Cornell to limb regeneration studies at Harvard—bring incredible prestige and authenticity to this initiative.

Our goal in this redesign is simple: Match ArtRX's digital presence with its real-world impact. By creating clear intake portals for hospital Child Life specialists, high school volunteer chapter toolkits, and senior-friendly accessibility features, ArtRX can easily scale alongside top student-founded organizations across the country.

Implementation Roadmap

Sequenced 4-Phase Development Plan

01
PHASE 1 · IMMEDIATE
Clean Static Prototype & Page Title Optimization
  • Optimize meta titles and descriptions across all pages to clearly reflect a non-profit youth charity.
  • Deploy the responsive static prototype (`index.html`) with zero slow tracking script overhead.
  • Integrate local high-resolution artwork and workshop photos in `assets/images/`.
02
PHASE 2 · WEEK 1
Facility Portals & Student Chapter Engine
  • Launch the dual intake forms: "Request Art Pads" (Hospitals) and "Schedule Workshop" (Senior Care).
  • Add the "Start an ArtRX Chapter" onboarding section with downloadable starter guides for high schools.
  • Apply the official ArtRX Color System (#1A365D Healing Navy + #FF6B6B Joyful Coral accents).
03
PHASE 3 · WEEKS 2–3
Senior Accessibility & Interactive Gallery
  • Incorporate high-contrast toggles and large font controls for senior citizens and elderly care coordinators.
  • Add interactive category filters to the Gallery (Pediatric Kits, Hospital Visits, Senior Workshops).
  • Highlight medical partner testimonials from physicians and attending specialists.
04
PHASE 4 · MONTH 1
Regional Expansion & Impact Mapping
  • Publish downloadable 1-page PDF flyers for student leaders to present to school boards and clubs.
  • Build the "Science of Creative Healing" section highlighting Thanvi's research background.
  • Integrate interactive hospital and nursing home impact location maps across New York.
