01 · What We Discussed

Your Vision & Key Requirements

In our discovery call, Thanvi and Dr. Sushma outlined the core goals, emotional purpose, and visual identity for ArtRX. Here is what we aligned on:

CORE MISSION

Comfort & Emotional Relief Through Drawing

ArtRX provides guided illustration prompt pads to children in hospital beds and conducts workshops for seniors in nursing homes to alleviate depression, loneliness, and anxiety.

VISUAL IDENTITY

Beige, Sage Green & Healing Navy

Thanvi selected a calm, non-clinical color scheme: Soft Beige (#FAF6F0) background, Healing Sage Green (#2F7A5C) accents, and Healing Navy (#1A365D) typography.

GALLERY LAYOUT

Segmented Templates vs. Patient Work

The gallery will feature 2 clear tabs: Guided Prompt Templates (Thanvi's custom drawing pads) and Real Patient Artwork (drawings created by kids and seniors).

CONTACT INTAKE

Dual Form Strategy

A simple form selector allowing visitors to reach out for General Inquiries, Student Volunteer Opportunities, or Hospital & Senior Facility Requests.

02 · What We Found in Research

12 Peer Benchmarks & National Gold Standards

To ensure ArtRX's new website matches the top student-led and healthcare art non-profits across the country, we analyzed 12 leading peer initiatives with verified active websites:

Teen-Founded Hospital Care

1. Kayla Cares 4 Kids

kaylacares4kids.org
Founded by 11-year-old Kayla, delivering 55,000+ art kits and educational items to pediatric hospital wards and Ronald McDonald Houses nationwide.
Key Finding: Hospital recipient directories and Junior Ambassador drive toolkits build instant credibility.
Global Student Network

2. Art Pass

artpass.org
A global high school student network spanning 24 chapters across 4 continents that distributes creative prompt cards and art kits to young patients.
Key Finding: Turnkey "Start an High School Chapter" PDF toolkits allow student initiatives to scale rapidly.
Youth Outreach

3. Cards for Hospitalized Kids

cardsforhospitalizedkids.com
Founded by 16-year-old Jen Rubino; reached 500,000+ hospitalized children with uplifting handmade art and cards.
Key Finding: Simple artwork and community drawing events create massive emotional resonance.
Hospital Creative Arts

4. Project Sunshine

projectsunshine.org
Delivers creative arts, play, and mental wellness kits directly to pediatric hospital wards across US medical centers.
Key Finding: Dedicated Child Life specialist request portals streamline hospital distribution.
Therapeutic Art

5. Draw Change

drawchange.org
Empowers hospitalized children through art therapy programs and self-expression workshops that foster emotional resilience.
Key Finding: Clear explanations of the psychological benefits of art therapy build donor trust.
Senior Care & Youth

6. Love For Our Elders

loveforourelders.org
Founded by student Jacob Cramer, mobilizing student volunteers to create handwritten artwork and letters for senior nursing home residents.
Key Finding: Matches Thanvi's senior care mission to combat elderly isolation through art.
Senior Companion Network

7. GlamourGals Foundation

glamourgals.org
Organizes high school student volunteer chapters to visit senior living centers for companionship, conversation, and creative sessions.
Key Finding: Excellent model for nursing home activity director scheduling portals.
Expression Journals

8. Art with Heart

artwithheart.org
Creates therapeutic art journals and expression drawing pads designed to help children express complex feelings during medical stays.
Key Finding: **Direct benchmark for Thanvi's illustration prompt pads!**
New York Arts Care

9. Free Arts NYC

freeartsnyc.org
A New York non-profit providing creative arts and mentoring programs to children across health centers and community centers in NYC.
Key Finding: Highlighting local New York hospital ties creates strong regional authority.
Participatory Painting

10. HospitalART Foundation

hospitalart.org
Donates paint-by-number artwork panels to healthcare facilities, allowing patients, families, and seniors to paint together.
Key Finding: Beautiful visual gallery layout for patient-created artwork.
Pediatric Oncology Art

11. Kids & Art Foundation

kidsandart.org
Provides free art workshops and destination art kits to pediatric oncology patients and care centers.
Key Finding: Physician and nurse testimonials validate program credibility.
Creative Healing Kits

12. Enchanted Makeovers

enchantedmakeovers.org
Distributes creative care kits, custom sketchbooks, and art supplies to pediatric centers and youth care facilities.
Key Finding: Packaging assurances and non-toxic safety details reassure hospital staff.

03 · What Will Work

Our Proposed Website Solution

Based on our discovery call and market research, here is the exact proposed website structure, technical stack, and design blueprint we are building for ArtRX:

1. HOME (`/`)

  • Hero Section: Bold headline + Dual CTAs ("Explore Gallery" & "Volunteer With Us").
  • Doctor Testimonial: Highlighted quote card from Dr. Venugopal (*Stars Surgical Suites*).
  • Pillars of Care: Dual cards for Pediatric Hospital Care + Senior Care Workshops.
  • Founder Spotlight: Thanvi's story & Weill Cornell / Harvard research credentials.

2. GALLERY (`/gallery`)

  • Segmented Tab Toggles: "Guided Templates" vs. "Patient Artwork".
  • Guided Templates Tab: Displays Thanvi's custom drawing prompt pads (*"Draw Your Family"*, *"Favorite Food"*, *"You & A Friend"*).
  • Patient Artwork Tab: Displays real completed drawings created by children in hospitals and senior nursing home residents.

3. GET INVOLVED (`/get-involved`)

  • Student Ambassador Section: Information for high school peers wanting to volunteer.
  • Future Chapter Toolkits: Downloadable PDF guides for starting ArtRX clubs in other high schools.

4. CONTACT (`/contact`)

  • Inquiry Selector: Dropdown choice for General Inquiry, Volunteer Interest, or Hospital/Senior Facility Request.
  • Direct Email Link: Connected to `artrx39@gmail.com`.
  • Social Media Links: Footer links to official Instagram & Facebook pages.
